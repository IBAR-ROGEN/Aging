# Results (draft excerpt)

## Longevity-associated candidate genes

We prioritized 41 genes at the intersection of AD/PD differential expression and
LongevityMap associations. Key loci include the cholesteryl ester transfer protein
(CETP) locus and the HLA class II DQ beta chain (DQB1 / DQB103 / DQB105 alleles).

Heat-shock cognate identifiers from older literature (HSP70-1, HSP70-1A, HSP70-1B,
HSP70-Hom) map onto the modern HGNC symbols HSPA1A/HSPA1B/HSPA1L and are retained
in the Protein Folding/HSP functional cluster.

Anti-longevity lipid genes CETP and APOC1, together with neuroinflammatory
regulators TLR4, LY86 and HLA-DQB1, form the primary down-regulated axis.
